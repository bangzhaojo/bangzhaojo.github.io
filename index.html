Research agenda

How can AI systems become more socially aware, interpretable, and safe?

01

Emotional & Social Intelligence

Human communication is shaped by emotions and social norms. I study how language models perceive, reason about, and respond to these contexts—and develop frameworks to evaluate their behavior.

02

Mechanistic Interpretability

Language models are often treated as black boxes. I investigate the internal mechanisms that give rise to model behavior, with the goal of improving interpretability and reliability.

03

Alignment & Safety

Reliable behavior aligned with human values is critical. I study evaluation and intervention methods that identify failure modes and guide models toward safer behavior.
